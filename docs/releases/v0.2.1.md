## Clear Liquid Glass and light-theme contrast

- Use the native clear Liquid Glass style instead of the heavier regular material.
- Apply the selected appearance to the native popup window as well as its glass views.
- Keep the light header, hint, and action buttons readable over dark desktops.
- Retain transparent, reflective wheel surfaces without the opaque frosted disk.

Includes Morphlet Studio, shared monochrome dark/light themes, image editing, and the Windows/X11 drag adapters introduced in v0.2.0. Wayland and unavailable monitors use the floating drop target. Packages remain unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized.
