## Wheel surface refinement

- Balance diffusion and contrast across light and dark wheel surfaces.
- Unify the header, hints, action buttons, preview and crop editor surfaces.
- Refine segment borders, typography and center contrast over desktop content.

Includes Morphlet Studio, image editing, batch conversion, native macOS glass, and portable drag adapters from v0.2.x. Wayland uses the floating drop-target fallback. Packages are unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized.
